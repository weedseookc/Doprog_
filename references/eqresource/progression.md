# The Burning Lands - Progression

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/progression.php

## Initial Access

Players start in **Stratos: Zephyr's Flight**, the only directly accessible zone initially. Note: "you can Campfire or Banner to bypass the Zone requirements."

## Ordered Progression Chain

### Phase 1: Stratos Foundation
1. Complete task: **Soldier of Air**
2. Complete group mission: **Fight Fire**
3. Unlock ability to request raid: **Fight Fire**

### Phase 2: Trials of Smoke
Complete at least one trial from these five options:
- Trial of the Ashes of Rusted Cliff's Glory
- Trial of the Eternal Cyclone
- Trial of the Speaker's Amphitheater
- Trial of the Wending Ways
- Trial of Three

**Important Note:** "With at least 1 Trial down, while also having it on Lockout, you will gain access to the next zone, Empyr: Realms of Ash." Full static zone access requires completing all 5 trials.

Unlocks raid: **Rise of Smoke**

### Phase 3: Empyr: Realms of Ash
1. **Prisoner's Dilemma** quest
2. **Palace of Embers** quest
3. Complete: **Fire and Fury** (for group mission access)

### Phase 4: Aalishai: Palace of Embers
1. **Brass Palace** quest
2. **Key to the Kingdom** quest

Grants access to: **Esianti: Palace of the Winds**

### Phase 5: Esianti: Palace of the Winds
Sequential quest chain:
1. **Contract of War**
2. **All Hail the King**
3. **Royal Visits**
4. **Tyrant of Fire**
5. **Enter Mearatas**
6. **Serving Another Master**

Unlocks final zone: **Mearatas: The Stone Demesne**

## Raid Progression Notes

**Doomfire raid** requires eight quests spanning multiple zones: "Prisoner's Dilemma," "Palace of Embers," "Brass Palace," "Key to the Kingdom," "Contract of War," "All Hail the King," "Royal Visits," and "Tyrant of Fire."

Final zone raids (Mearatas/Aalishai) require "21 clicks of Tier 2 raid flags."

## Zone Access Mechanics

Lockout status gates progression. A user comment states the main text "needs updated. you only gain access while you have the lockout."
