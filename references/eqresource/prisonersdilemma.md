# Prisoner's Dilemma

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/prisonersdilemma.php

## Quest Giver
**Star Stealing Sage** - Located in Empyr: Realms of Ash (exact coordinates not provided on page).
Note: "Must kill the 3 guards around him first"

## Request Phrase
"could"

## Requirements
- Soldier of Air (quest)
- Fight Fire (quest)
- Any Trials of Smoke (quest)

## Quest Properties
- **Zone:** Empyr: Realms of Ash
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Time Limit:** Unlimited
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Task Objectives (IN ORDER)
1. "Intercept messenger going to the warfront. Find any information you can. 0/1"
2. "Intercept messenger going coming from the warfront. Find any information you can. 0/1"
3. "Intercept messenger that travels the towers. Find any information you can. 0/1"
4. "Question, if you can, Blazing Battleworn Eyes. 0/1"
5. "Bring the message to the warfront to Star Stealing Sage. 0/1"
6. "Bring the message from the warfront to Star Stealing Sage. 0/1"
7. "Bring the messages from the Empyr towers to Star Stealing Sage. Tell him about what you learned"
8. "Speak with Great Sky Ocean about the War. 0/1" (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)

## Mechanics (from Player Comments)

**Blazing Battleworn Eyes interaction:** Located in "South East most section of the zone." After engaging: "attack him after he says die then, when drops aggro tell him 'If you agree to tell us more we will let you go'"

**Messenger Locations:** One player noted: "third bag is the messenger that moves from entrance south to the spot where you get Task." Another stated: "you get 2 updates fast in the valley North of the guards."

**Group Mechanics Issue:** When multiple players speak to Great Sky Ocean together, "everyone's quest updated, but only one person could use the [news] prompt to get the next quest." Solution: "disbanded the group, which allowed each person to continue."
