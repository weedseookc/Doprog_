# Serving Another Master

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/servinganothermaster.php

## Quest Details

- **Quest Giver:** Obsidian Sundering Master (Esianti: Palace of the Winds)
- **Request Phrase:** "can do"
- **Requirements:** Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, Contract of War, All Hail the King, Royal Visits, Tyrant of Fire, and Enter Mearatas
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 24 hours
- **Lockout:** 5 hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objectives (In Order)

1. Speak with Obsidian Sundering Master. 0/1
2. Speak with Silent Sun 0/1
3. Find the being called Ghost Ruby. 0/1
4. Defeat Ghost Ruby and take his head. 0/1
5. Give the head of Ghost Ruby to Silent Sun. 0/1
6. Deliver the Dark Ruby Muhbis to Obsidian Sundering Master. 0/1
7. Deliver the Elegant Note to Obsidian Sundering Master. 0/1

## Critical Steps

- Say **'where is Ghost Ruby?'** to fire area mobs in the NW zone area
- Ghost Ruby spawns ~30 seconds later at a random building location
- Loot **Head of Ghost Ruby** from corpse
- Turn head to Silent Sun (receives **Dark Ruby Muhbis** and **Elegant Note**)
- Return both items to Obsidian Sundering Master

## Rewards

- 283 Platinum, 3 Gold, 3 Silver, 3 Copper
- Experience
