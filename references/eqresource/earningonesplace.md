# Earning One's Place

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/earningonesplace.php

## Quest Info
- **Quest Name:** Earning One's Place
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Iron Lightning Spirit (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)
- **Request Phrase:** "worthiness"
- **Requirements:** Servants of Esianti faction at "Threateningly or Lower"
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes
- **Time Limit:** Unlimited

## Task Objective
1. "Gain favor with the Djinn by assisting in their war with the Efreeti. 0/5" (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)

## Walkthrough
- Defeat mobs located "by the Plane of Smoke zone area"

## Rewards
- Increases faction with Servants of Esianti
- Achievement: "Mercenary of Stratos: Zephyr's Flight"

## Player Comments
- One player noted confusion about identifying the Djinn faction in the faction window, suggesting "the NPC was on the esianti faction in Beta" but may now use neutral factions.
- Tip: "Say 'accept' to the Soaring Moon npc to port over, hide behind tree if kos" (kill on sight).
