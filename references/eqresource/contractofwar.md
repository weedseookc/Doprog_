# Contract of War

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/contractofwar.php

**Quest Name:** Contract of War

**Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Great Sky Ocean (located in Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)

**Request Phrase:** "mission"

**Zone-in Phrase:** "to go"

**Time Limit:** 6 Hours

**Lockout:** 5 Hours

**Task Type:** Group (1 Minimum, 6 Maximum Players)

**Repeatable:** Yes

## Requirements
Players must complete: Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, and Key to the Kingdom

## Task Objectives (In Order)

1. "Find and defeat whomever is palace guardian today and acquire a writ to see the king. 0/1"
2. "Speak with the king about the war. 0/1"
3. "Open the Chest. 0/1"

## Key Named Mobs
- Sunshine Sensible Warmth
- Radiant Fog Evening
- Feather Silver Sheen

These spawn in random order and are "tethered to their spawn point."

## Critical Mechanics
Players must "drop the expedition but stay in the task" and "log out to player select and back in" to prevent mob despawning. After defeating the final named, click the Moon Serf door, defeat spawning mephits, then say "do you know who will follow" to Moon Serf of Harmonious Heavens.

## Rewards
- Experience
- "212 Platinum, 5 Gold"
- "96x Fettered Ifrit Coins"
