# Fragmented Coterie

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/fragmentedcoterie.php

## Overview

- **Quest Name:** Fragmented Coterie
- **Quest Giver:** Spear Sundering Ivory (Aalishai: Palace of Embers)
- **Request Phrase:** "exile"
- **Requirements:** Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, and Palace of Embers
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 5 Hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes
- **Time Limit:** Unlimited

## Objectives (in order)

1. "Speak to Ebon Ice Servant and deliver Spear Sundering Ivory's First Confession 0/1" (Timorous Deep)
2. "Speak to Towering Silhouette and deliver Spear Sundering Ivory's Second Confession 0/1" (South Desert of Ro)
3. "Speak to Alabaster Rose Moon and deliver Spear Sundering Ivory's Third Confession 0/1" (Barren Coast)
4. "Speak to Shadow Metal Guardian and deliver Spear Sundering Ivory's Final Confession 0/1" (The Stonebrunt Mountains)
5. "Return to Spear Sundering Ivory and tell him what had happened 0/1" (Aalishai: Palace of Embers)

## NPCs

- Spear Sundering Ivory (Aalishai: Palace of Embers) - quest giver / final turn-in
- Ebon Ice Servant (Timorous Deep)
- Towering Silhouette (South Desert of Ro)
- Alabaster Rose Moon (Barren Coast)
- Shadow Metal Guardian (The Stonebrunt Mountains)

## Items

- Spear Sundering Ivory's Third Confession -> Alabaster Rose Moon
- Spear Sundering Ivory's Final Confession -> Shadow Metal Guardian
