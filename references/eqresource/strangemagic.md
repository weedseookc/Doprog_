# Strange Magic

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/strangemagic.php

## Overview
- **Quest Name:** Strange Magic
- **Quest Giver / Start:** Click on Door in Relic, the Artifact City
- **Zone:** Relic, the Artifact City
- **Request / Zone-In Phrase:** Click on Door in Relic, the Artifact City
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 6 Hours
- **Lockout:** 5 Hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Requirements (prerequisite tasks)
Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, Contract of War, All Hail the King, Royal Visits, Tyrant of Fire, Enter Mearatas, Serving Another Master, Earthen Dirge, Mold Seeker, Relic Raider, and Delivery.

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "Use the Infused Sandstone Siphon to gather magic from the room. 0/1"
2. "Defeat the guardians. 0/4"
3. "Use the Infused Sandstone Siphon to gather magic from the room. 0/1"
4. "Open the chest. 0/1"

## Items / Clickables
- Infused Sandstone Siphon (right-click / "Use" to gather magic from the room)

## Key Mechanics (from walkthroughs)
**Step 1:** Right-click the Siphon provided at task start.

**Step 2 - Four Guardians:**
- One applies "Life and Death" debuff - players must "hug each other".
- One applies "Static" - players must "run away from each other".
- Each guardian death spawns adds (2, 3, 4, and 5 respectively).
- Spellbinding Guardian does "Zone wide AE that negates all spell haste".
- All mobs are rootable, stunnable, and mezzable.
- Adds are mezzable and charming is possible with certain abilities.

**Step 3:** Defeat adds, then right-click Siphon.

**Step 4:** Open the chest.
