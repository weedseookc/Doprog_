# Slippery Slope

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/slipperyslope.php

**Quest Name:** Slippery Slope

**Quest Giver:** Flexing Devout Purpose

**Zone:** Mearatas: The Stone Demesne

**Request Phrase:** "clues"

**Task Type:** Solo

**Time Limit:** Unlimited

**Lockout:** 30 Minutes

**Repeatable:** Yes

---

## Objectives (In Order):

1. "'Convince' aqua envoys to give up any information they may have about the ondine's schemes 0/1"
2. "Deliver the Damp Missive to Flexing Devout Purpose to confirm his suspicions 0/1"
3. "Locate Emli Widgetton and give her the Intriguing Damp Missive 0/1"
4. "Bring the Translated Damp Missive back to Flexing Devout Purpose 0/1"
5. "Confront Unrepentant Bronze Temper with evidence from the Cryptic Damp Missive and get her to confess 0/1"
6. "Find evidence of Unrepentant Bronze Temper's scheme 0/1"
7. "Take the Soggy Journal to Flexing Devout Purpose 0/1"

---

## Key NPCs & Items:

- **Flexing Devout Purpose** (quest giver/turn-in)
- **Emli Widgetton** (translation NPC)
- **Unrepentant Bronze Temper** (boss - aggros after confrontation)
- **Damp Missive** (looted from aqua envoys, southeastern zone)
- **Soggy Journal** (looted from defeated Unrepentant Bronze Temper)

---

## Player Comments:

"Each step that has a dropped item should drop one for each person in the group doing the quest."
