# Earthen Dirge

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/earthendirge.php

**Quest Name:** Earthen Dirge

**Start NPC:** Obsidian Sundering Master (Esianti: Palace of the Winds)

**Request Phrase:** "forget"

**Task Type:** Solo | **Lockout:** 30 Minutes | **Repeatable:** Yes

---

## Objectives (In Order)

1. "Convince Dirge in the Catacomb to let you use his portal. 0/1 (Esianti: Palace of the Winds)"
   - Go to Dirge in Catacomb and say 'something'

2. "Defeat Funereal Dust Warrior and recover his head. 0/1 (Mearatas: The Stone Demesne)"
   - Defeat the mob at marked location

3. "Deliver 1 Head of Funereal Dust Warrior to Dirge in the Catacomb 0/1"
   - Return head to Dirge in Esianti

---

## Reward

141 Platinum, 6 Gold, 6 Silver, 7 Copper + Experience
