# Mold Seeker

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/moldseeker.php

**Quest Name:** Mold Seeker

**Quest Giver:** Obsidian Sundering Master (Esianti: Palace of the Winds)

**Request Phrase:** "mold seeker"

**Zone:** Esianti: Palace of the Winds / Mearatas: The Stone Demesne

**Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)

**Time Limit:** 24 Hours

**Lockout:** 30 Minutes

**Repeatable:** Yes

---

## Prerequisites
Requires completion of: Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, Contract of War, All Hail the King, Royal Visits, Tyrant of Fire, Enter Mearatas, Serving Another Master, and Earthen Dirge.

---

## Objectives (In Order)

1. "Find out where the duende keep their unused female mold. 0/1" (Mearatas: The Stone Demesne)
2. "Speak with the relic keeper and see if you can find out where the mold is kept. 0/1"
3. "Get the relic keeper's key. 0/1"
4. "Pick up the relic keeper's key. 0/1"

---

## Walkthrough Steps

**Step 1:** Say "where is the female duende mold" to duende mobs in the Northeastern section; some will aggro.

**Step 2:** Locate Battleworn Obelisk and say "show me the relics"—mob will aggro.

**Step 3:** Defeat Battleworn Obelisk.

**Step 4:** Loot the key.

---

## Rewards
Experience and "141 Platinum, 6 Gold, 6 Silver, 7 Copper"

**Achievement:** Partisan of Mearatas: The Stone Demesne
