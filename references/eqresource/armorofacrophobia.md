Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/armorofacrophobia.php

**Quest Name:** Armor of Acrophobia

**Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Phibbit in Esianti: Palace of the Winds

**Request Phrase:** "crag"

**Requirements:** Completion of Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, any Trials of Smoke quest, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, and Key to the Kingdom

**Task Type:** Solo | **Time Limit:** Unlimited | **Lockout:** 30 Minutes | **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "Free Crag Armors 0/4 (Esianti: Palace of the Winds)"
2. "Return to Phibbit 0/1 (Esianti: Palace of the Winds)"

## Task Details
- Defeat crag armors located throughout the Southwest section of the zone
- Return to and hail Phibbit at the marked location

**Rewards:** 212 Platinum, 5 Gold

**Achievement:** Mercenary of Esianti: Palace of the Winds
