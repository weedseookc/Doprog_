# Soldier of Air

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/soldierofair.php

## Quest Info
- **Quest Name:** Soldier of Air
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Grieving Soul Scent (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)
- **Request Phrase:** "deal with"
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes
- **Time Limit:** Unlimited

## Task Objectives (In Order)
1. "Drive back the invaders from Empyr. 0/5 (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)"
2. "Speak with Grieving Soul Scent 0/1 (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)"

## Walkthrough Steps
- Defeat mobs at Plane of Smoke zone area
- Return to and hail Grieving Soul Scent

## Rewards
- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold

## Achievements Unlocked
- Mercenary of Stratos: Zephyr's Flight
- Trials of Smoke (Group)
- Adventurer of The Burning Lands (Group)
- Strategic Entry (Group)
