# Remodeling

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/remodeling.php

## Overview
- **Quest Name:** Remodeling
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Unrepentant Sunrise (location coordinates not provided on page)
- **Zone:** Doomfire, the Burning Lands
- **Request Phrase:** "remodeling"
- **Zone-In Phrase:** "ready"
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 6 Hours
- **Lockout:** 5 Hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Requirements (prerequisite tasks)
Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, Contract of War, All Hail the King, Royal Visits, Tyrant of Fire, Enter Mearatas, Serving Another Master, Earthen Dirge, Mold Seeker, Relic Raider, Delivery, and Strange Magic.

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "Fight your way to the throne room to speak with Fennin Ro" (0/4)
2. "Deliver 1 Saturated Infused Sandstone Siphon to Fennin Ro" (0/1)
3. "Open the chest" (0/1)

## Notable Mobs / Bosses
- Jopal see-invis mobs (zone-in area)
- Guardian of Doomfire (leashed; regens if out of range)
- Two Snails (eastern area)
- General Reparm (Fennin's room)

## Items
- Saturated Infused Sandstone Siphon (delivered to Fennin Ro)

## Turn-In
Fennin Ro (throne room) - deliver 1 Saturated Infused Sandstone Siphon.

## Rewards
- Experience
- 318 Platinum, 7 Gold, 5 Silver
- 143x Fettered Ifrit Coins
- Escargot Anneau De Feu (chest loot)
- Stave of Earth Sundering (chest loot)

## Player Comments / Mechanics Notes
- Guardian of Doomfire "will Doom a random player, this player will probably die when the debuff fades."
- General Reparm: "run away from group to not viral them if you get emoted."
