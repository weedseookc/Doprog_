## The Core Mechanic: Counting Portals (Kill Order)

The trial presents four elemental **ambassadors**. The order you must fight them in is
**not fixed** — it is determined each run by counting the colored steam/portals around the area.

**Exact rule (EQ Resource walkthrough), verbatim:**
> "The most portal types you see around the area, determines which ambassador to fight next."

Supplemental walkthrough note (EQ Resource):
> "You can also invis past the trash mobs"

**Portal / steam color → element mapping** (from user **Marnic**, Jan 25 2019), verbatim:
> "White Steam = Lightning, Red Steam = Fire, Blue Steam = Water, Green Steam = Earth"

| Steam / Portal Color | Element | Ambassador |
|---|---|---|
| **White** | Lightning / **Air (Wind)** | Blustering Stalwart Screen |
| **Red** | **Fire** | Blazing Triumphant Bulwark |
| **Blue** | **Water** | Flowing Unconquered Guard |
| **Green** | **Earth** | Obsidian Undefeated Shield |

**How to read it:** Count which element's portals are most numerous; that element's ambassador
is your next target. User **Theolas** phrases it concretely:
> "whichever one has seven portals is the mob you need to kill"

User **Ophyllia** (Jan 3 2019) clarifies the rule against a common misreading:
> "It is based on the most portal types of that element"
> *(i.e. it is the element with the MOST portals, not simply the first portal you walk past.)*

After you defeat each ambassador, re-count the remaining portals to find the next target.

**Navigation note (EQ Resource):**
> "After killing each mob the portals will all eventually take you back to the entrance
> platform with the 3 jump portals[, so] no mistake is possible"

## The Four Ambassadors

### 1. Blazing Triumphant Bulwark — FIRE (hardest)

This is widely reported as the hardest of the four ("take forever to kill, especially the
Fire one" — **Battileur**). The boss is normally **immune/insubstantial** and must be dragged
onto the **burning areas / braziers (fire pits)** to become vulnerable.

**The drag-to-fire mechanic (EQ Resource walkthrough), VERBATIM:**
> "Blazing Triumphant Bulwark = fire - move him to the burning areas until the emote about
> changing back, he will need to be moved between the fire areas"

**Solidity / insubstantial emotes (verbatim):**
> "Blazing Triumphant Bulwark gains solidity."
> "Blazing Triumphant Bulwark becomes insubstantial, locked into his current form."

**Mechanic explained (user Yraen, Dec 12 2018):**
> "Drag NPC inside brazier to receive emotes"
> — i.e. position the boss inside/over a fire pit; he gains solidity (becomes damageable);
> after some damage he becomes insubstantial again ("locked into his current form") and must
> be dragged to the **next** fire pit to resume taking damage.

**Health-percentage lock points** — sources disagree slightly on the exact numbers, so both
are recorded:
- **Theolas:** "At 57% he will lock and you have to move him to another pit. At 24% he locks again."
- **Vumad** (Dec 17 2018): "On fire, the 25% is not accurate. We had our lock at 56%."

> **Practical strategy (synthesized from EQ Resource + ZAM):** Stand behind/near a fire pit,
> tag the boss so he runs onto the pit, hold him there ~5 seconds for damage to register,
> then move him to a new pit at roughly **~57% (or ~56%)**, and again at roughly **~24% (or ~25%)**,
> finishing him at the final pit. He must be cycled between **multiple** fire areas; one pit
> is not enough.

---

### 2. Obsidian Undefeated Shield — EARTH (split)

**Split mechanic (EQ Resource walkthrough), VERBATIM:**
> "Obsidian Undefeated Shield = earth - at 50% splits into 2 adds"

At 50% health the boss splits into two adds:
- **Obsidian Fragment**
- **Obsidian Shard** — **MEZZABLE** (mez/CC the Shard, kill the Fragment, then finish the Shard).

User **Yraen** confirms verbatim:
> "Obsidian Shard: Mezzable"

User **Theolas** also notes the "SHARD" during the earth split is mezzable.

---

### 3. Flowing Unconquered Guard — WATER (tank-and-spank)

**EQ Resource walkthrough, verbatim:**
> "Flowing Unconquered Guard = water"

Reported as a relatively straightforward tank-and-spank. User **Yraen** notes the water boss
**casts a healing damage-dealer** ("Water Draw"–style single-target DD that heals the NPC),
so it is somewhat resistant / slow to kill, but it has no complex scripted phases.

---

### 4. Blustering Stalwart Screen — WIND / AIR (tank-and-spank)

**EQ Resource walkthrough, verbatim:**
> "Blustering Stalwart Screen = wind"

Another straightforward tank-and-spank but **very resistant**. Notable detail from **Vumad**:
> air "cannot be slowed" (does not take slow). Uses a point-blank AE ("Static Expulsion"–style).

---

### Final Step

**EQ Resource walkthrough, verbatim:**
> "Open chest to complete trial"

## Achievements

The EQ Resource page lists four achievements for this trial:

1. **Trials of Smoke (Group)** — the overall completion achievement for the group trials.
2. **Appointments Required** — (the standard "complete the trial" / fight-all-ambassadors achievement).
3. **Don't Trip** — relates to navigating the **jump/platform portals at the entrance** without
   bouncing/falling.
4. **Just Passing Through** — requires hitting the **flying Nilborian** while jumping.

**Achievement notes from comments:**

- **Don't Trip** (the entrance jump-portal puzzle): there are **3 jump portals** on the entrance
  platform. EQ Resource notes:
  > "Falling into the middle will port you to a safe spot, but costs you an achievement."
  - **Tucoh** (Mar 11 2019) describes it as a luck mechanic: "it's just a probabilistic failure
    mechanic you need to keep rolling the dice against" — and reports someone needing **49 attempts**.
  - **Marnic** notes the **landing/jump mechanic is buggy**, making these achievements partly luck.
  - **Riou** (Dec 11 2018) notes "Don't Trip" is for **zero bounces**.

- **Just Passing Through** (EQ Resource / Marnic), verbatim:
  > "For Just Passing Through you have to hit the flying nilborian while jumping."

## Quick Strategy Checklist

1. Talk to **Waves of Saffron Sky**, say `prepared`.
2. (Optional) For **Don't Trip** / **Just Passing Through**: clear the **3 entrance jump portals**
   without bouncing, and hit the **flying Nilborian** mid-jump. These are luck-heavy and buggy.
3. **Count the steam/portals** around the area; the element with the **most** portals
   (≈ seven) is the next ambassador. Invis past trash if desired.
4. Kill that ambassador:
   - **Fire (Blazing Triumphant Bulwark):** drag onto fire pits; he "gains solidity" (damageable);
     when he "becomes insubstantial, locked into his current form" (~57%/56% and ~24%/25%),
     drag him to the next pit. Hardest fight.
   - **Earth (Obsidian Undefeated Shield):** at 50% splits into **Obsidian Fragment** +
     **Obsidian Shard**; **mez the Shard**, kill Fragment, then the Shard.
   - **Water (Flowing Unconquered Guard):** tank-and-spank; self-heals via a healing DD.
   - **Wind/Air (Blustering Stalwart Screen):** tank-and-spank; very resistant; **cannot be slowed**.
5. Re-count portals, repeat for the remaining ambassadors. Portals route you back to the
   entrance platform between fights.
6. **Open the chest** to complete the trial.
