# Trial of the Wending Ways
Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/trialofthewendingways.php

## Start NPC
- **Waves of Saffron Sky** (location marked with smallblackboxcb.png on map)
- Zone: The Plane of Smoke (must be inside Trials of Smoke instance)
- Request Phrase: "prepared"

## Quest Type
- Group (1-6 players), 6-hour time limit, 60-hour lockout, repeatable

## Objectives (In Order)
- "Defeat any trial that you have not already defeated and claim your reward. 0/1 (Plane of Smoke)"

## Bosses & Mechanics
The four elemental bosses are:
1. **Blazing Triumphant Bulwark** (Fire) - "move him to the burning areas until the emote about changing back"
2. **Obsidian Undefeated Shield** (Earth) - "at 50% splits into 2 adds"
3. **Flowing Unconquered Guard** (Water)
4. **Blustering Stalwart Screen** (Wind)

## Kill Order Determination
- "The most portal types you see around the area, determines which ambassador to fight next."
- Portal colors: white (air), red (fire), blue (water), green (earth).

## Rewards
- Experience
- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold
- Chest loot including: Diminished Inhabited Muhbis, Dour Blue, Happy Stone, Spellbound Lamps, daggers, rings, and Muhbis armor pieces
