# Fire and Fury

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/fireandfury.php

## Quest Giver
**Horizon Blighted Sage** (location indicated by a small black box on the map)

## Zone
Empyr: Realms of Ash

## Request Phrase
"curious"

## Requirements
- Soldier of Air
- Fight Fire
- Any Trials of Smoke

## Quest Properties
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes
- **Time Limit:** Unlimited

## Task Objectives (IN ORDER)
1. "Kill 3 Wildfire Phoenix 0/3 (Empyr: Realms of Ash)"
2. "Reveal the Truth 0/1 (Empyr: Realms of Ash)"
3. "Deliver 1 Rebirth of Heaven's Truth Wand to Horizon Blighted Sage 0/1"
4. "Speak with Horizon Blighted Sage about the [truth] 0/1"

## Walkthrough Details
- **Step 1:** Wildfire Phoenixes spawn "in the areas around the quest giver"
- **Step 2:** Use the item given on task request and click it on phoenixes. "It is best to wait until they are low before clicking." If successful, a lava elemental spawns - defeat it.
- **Step 3:** Return wand to Horizon Blighted Sage
- **Step 4:** Say "truth" to NPC, then view his dialogue

## Items
- Rebirth of Heaven's Truth Wand

## Rewards
- Experience
- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold

## Player Comments
"Only certain phoenixes count. One of the 4 immediately visible from Horizon counts."
