# All Hail the King

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/allhailtheking.php

## Starting NPC

- **Great Sky Ocean** (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)
- **Request Phrase:** "work"
- **Zone:** Stratos: Zephyr's Flight

## Quest Requirements

Must complete: Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, and Contract of War

## Task Details

- **Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 24 hours
- **Lockout:** 30 minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objectives (In Order)

1. Speak with Silent White Sky; say "support for the king" (map marker 1)
2. Deliver three writs to Silent White Sky:
   - Hail Still Sky's Scholar (marker 2), defeat, loot writ
   - Hail Hidden Winds (marker 3), defeat, loot writ
   - Hail Wistful Silken Fist (marker 4), defeat, loot writ
   - Turn all three to Silent White Sky
3. Speak with Maiden of Nine Roses; say "support for the king" (marker 5)
4. Defeat jopal in NW area; loot clickable; use in SW building (mobs aggro)
5. Right-click Nine Rose Dust at waterfalls (marker 6)
6. Right-click Nine Rose Dust on five Brume Armors throughout zone
7. Defeat jopal; loot clicky; use on Norrathians in SE building
8. Turn Rose Dust to Maiden of Nine Roses (marker 5)
9. Speak with Daughter of Invincible Sun; say "support for the king" (marker 7)
10. Say "insult" near Rememberance Consuming Sage when mob present; DPS to 9%
11. Hail Daughter of Invincible Sun (marker 7)
12. Speak with Dance of Metal and Ivory; say "support for the king" (marker 8)
13. Craft ONE of:
    - **Everwarm Bread:** Combine 4 Phoenix Dough (from Plane of Air phoenix eggs) + 2 Unebbing Embers (NW fire mobs) + Bread Tin in Oven
    - **Jagged Emblem Platinum Amulet:** Combine Emblem of Storms (PoP drops) + Platinum Bar + 2 Lightning Essences (air mobs, Brume Armor drops) in Jewel Crafting container
    - Turn to Dance of Metal and Ivory (marker 8)
14. Turn four support notes to Sky Orchid Understanding (marker 7)

## Rewards

- Experience
- 425 Platinum

## Player Comments

"Draught of the Craftsman strongly recommended; farming materials took hours with" repeated failures even with trophies.

Multiple council members exist; "Rememberance Consuming Sage is NOT always the one" that updates the task.

Lightning essences drop from "Brume Armors" during grinding.

When lighting fire, mobs aggro but "do NOT need to be killed for the update."

Some players report insult mechanic occasionally fails; "drag him to any zone trash NPC" and retry the phrase.
