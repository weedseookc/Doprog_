## Quest Info
- **Quest Name:** Do Unto Them
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Ashen Wandering Horizon (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)
- **Request Phrase:** "destroy the djinn"
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes
- **Time Limit:** Unlimited

## Task Objectives (In Order)
