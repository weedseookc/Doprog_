# Trial of Three
Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/trialofthree.php

## Start NPC
- **Waves of Saffron Sky** (location marked with smallblackboxcb.png on map)
- Zone: The Plane of Smoke
- Request Phrase: "prepared"
- Requirements: Must be inside the Trials of Smoke instance

## Task Details
- Time Limit: 6 Hours
- Task Type: Group, 1-6 players
- Lockout: 60 Hours
- Repeatable: Yes

## Objectives (In Order)
- "Defeat any trial that you have not already defeated and claim your reward. 0/1 (Plane of Smoke)"

## Bosses & Mechanics
Three mobs with elemental themes:
- "Dark Waters Sing" (Water element)
- "Warm Heart Flickers" (Fire element)
- "Shadows of Stone" (Earth element)

## Kill Order Determination
- Players receive four clue lines from the NPC. "You will get clues for the first and 3rd mob to defeat, the 2nd mob to defeat has no clues related to it."
- Clues reference: weapon type, mob size, position in room, or elemental matching.

## Turn-In
- Open chest to complete

## Rewards
- Experience
- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold
- Chest loot (13 possible items listed)

## Player Comments
- "stay there and do not move from the hailed mob"
- "if you do not ALL go into the room at the same time you will be kicked out / blinded"
- Mobs can "leash" if pushed to room edges.
- Boss max melee hits: 35-40k damage.
