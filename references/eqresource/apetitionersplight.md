# A Petitioner's Plight

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/apetitionersplight.php

## Quest Info
- **Quest Name:** A Petitioner's Plight
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Iron Lightning Spirit (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)
- **Request Phrase:** "aid"
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 24 Hours
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Task Objectives (In Order)
1. "Remove unwanted petitioners. 0/4 (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)"
2. "Find and get rid of some of the more persistent petitioners. 0/2"
3. "Speak with Iron Lightning Spirit about what you have accomplished. 0/1"

## Task Details
**Step 1:** Defeat petitioners found throughout the zone.

**Step 2:** Options include:
- Head to Still Ores location (marked on map), go through text, defeat twice
- Defeat Still Ores and Bloom together (marked on map)
- Say "karana," proceed through Cold Cloud Blossom text (marked on map)

**Step 3:** Return and hail Iron Lightning Spirit.

## Rewards
- Experience
- "283 Platinum, 3 Gold, 3 Silver, 3 Copper"
- Achievement: Partisan of Stratos: Zephyr's Flight

## Player Comments
- To access the NPC, "hail Soaring Moon and say 'assist'" to reach Iron Lightning Spirit location.
