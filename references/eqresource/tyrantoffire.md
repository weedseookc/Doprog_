# Tyrant of Fire

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/tyrantoffire.php

## Overview
- **Quest Name:** Tyrant of Fire
- **Quest Giver / Start:** Golden Ruby and Garnet Ring (right-click in the Plane of Tranquility)
- **Zone:** The Plane of Tranquility / Doomfire, the Burning Lands
- **Request Phrase:** "Click on the Golden Ruby and Garnet Ring, if you need another you can get one from Sky Orchid Understanding in Esianti"
- **Zone-In Phrase:** "ready to Unrepentant Sunrise in Plane of Fire"
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 6 Hours
- **Lockout:** 5 Hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Requirements (prerequisite tasks)
Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, Contract of War, All Hail the King, Royal Visits.

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "Speak with Fennin Ro about the ring. 0/1 (Doomfire, the Burning Lands)"
2. "Open the chest. 0/1 (Doomfire, the Burning Lands)"

## Items / Clickables
- Golden Ruby and Garnet Ring (right-click to start; replacement available from Sky Orchid Understanding in Esianti)

## Mechanics
"Head South, into the courtyard, defeat the invader mobs, in packs throughout the area...defeat Tank mobs first since they summon via script...mobs can be CC'd and aren't KOS."

## Turn-In
Speak with Fennin Ro about the ring (Doomfire, the Burning Lands).

## Rewards
- Experience
- 318 Platinum, 7 Gold, 5 Silver
- 143x Fettered Ifrit Coins
- Chest Loot includes: Assassin's Kerchief of Eventide, Battleworn Stalwart Moon Binding items, Empyrean Lamp of Ashes, Median Spellbound Lamp
