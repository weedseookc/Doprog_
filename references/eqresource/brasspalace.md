# Brass Palace

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/brasspalace.php

**Quest Name:** Brass Palace

**Quest Giver:** Great Sky Ocean (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)

**Request Phrase:** "see it through"

**Zone-in Phrase:** "ready"

**Requirements:** Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, and Palace of Embers

**Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)

**Time Limit:** 6 Hours

**Lockout:** 5 Hours

**Repeatable:** Yes

## Task Objectives (In Order)

1. "Defeat the guards and get into the palace. 0/1"
2. "Defeat the guards and get into the throne room. 0/1"
3. "Defeat the guards and get into the queen's bedroom. 0/1"
4. "Find evidence of the queen's involvement in the war between djinn and efreeti. 0/1"
5. "Open the chest. 0/1"

## Walkthrough Steps

- Defeat pyreglare armors until Silent Sun spawns; defeat it
- Defeat mobs until Ash Wrathful spawns; avoid fire elementals
- Continue east defeating mobs until Discipline of Flame spawns
- Pick up "Note on Brass" from queen's bedroom table; right-click it
- Open the chest

## Boss HP (from comments)

- Base population: 13 million HP
- Silent Sun: 55 million HP
- Ash Wrathful: 55 million HP
- Discipline of Flame: 55 million HP

Blue Sky, Conquering Blade of Stars, Happy Stone, Lesser Spellbound Lamp, Mask of the Sky Bladesman, Median Spellbound Lamp, Mortal Corporeal Light Zephyr, Pocket of Clouds, Weeping Undefeated Heaven Binding pieces (Chest, Head, Legs, Wrist)
