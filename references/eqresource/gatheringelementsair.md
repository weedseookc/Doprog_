# Gathering Elements: Air

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/gatheringelementsair.php

**Quest Name:** "Gathering Elements: Air"

**Start NPC:** Everna Delestrod (located in Aalishai: Palace of Embers)

**Request Phrase:** "take advantage"

**Zone:** Aalishai: Palace of Embers

**Requirements:** Completion of Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, and Palace of Embers

**Task Type:** Solo | **Time Limit:** Unlimited | **Lockout:** 30 Minutes | **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objective

- "Deliver 10 Essence of Elemental Air to Everna Delestrod 0/10"

## Walkthrough

Defeat air-related mobs in the northern section of the zone to obtain Essence of Elemental Air items, then return and turn in to Everna Delestrod.

## Rewards

- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold
- Achievement: Mercenary of Aalishai: Palace of Embers
