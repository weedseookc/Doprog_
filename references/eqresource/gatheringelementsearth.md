# Gathering Elements: Earth

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/gatheringelementsearth.php

**Quest Giver:** Everna Delestrod
**Zone:** Aalishai: Palace of Embers
**Request Phrase:** "take advantage"

**Requirements:** Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Gathering Elements: Air, and Gathering Elements: Water

**Task Type:** Solo
**Time Limit:** Unlimited
**Lockout:** 30 Minutes
**Repeatable:** Yes

## Objectives

- "Deliver 10 Essence of Elemental Earth to Everna Delestrod 0/10"

## Walkthrough

Defeat earth-related mobs located throughout the Eastern section of the zone to obtain essences, then return them to Everna Delestrod (marked on map).

## Rewards

- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold
- Achievement: Mercenary of Aalishai: Palace of Embers
