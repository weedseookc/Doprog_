# Palace of Embers

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/palaceofembers.php

## Quest Giver
**Great Sky Ocean** (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)

## Request Phrase
"news"

## Requirements
- Soldier of Air
- Fight Fire
- Any Trials of Smoke
- Prisoner's Dilemma

## Quest Properties
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Task Objectives (IN ORDER)
1. "Find someone that you can force to give you access to Aalishai. 0/1 (Empyr: Realms of Ash)"
2. "Find someone that you can force to give you access to the palace. 0/1 (Aalishai: Palace of Embers)"
3. "You will have to kill the ambassador to prevent him from reporting what you have done. 0/1"
4. "Return the challenge to Great Sky Ocean. 0/1 (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)"

## Mechanics (from Player Comments)
- **Step 1:** Kill mobs of the same type (spiders spawn Flame Consuming Hand). Named de-aggros at 10% and requires the phrase "KILL YOU" to trigger combat.
- **Step 2:** Kill water/fire/air/earth mobs in Aalishai to spawn ambassador. Named requires phrase "entry" at 10%.
- Ambassadors include: All Consuming Sound (Water), Descending Light's Laughter (Earth), Warrior's Flowing Mind (Fire)
- Named mobs will despawn if combat takes too long
- Step 2 named has "nasty fire dots" and can mez tanks

## Reward
- Experience
- 354 Platinum, 1 Gold, 6 Silver, 7 Copper
