# Gathering Elements: Water

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/gatheringelementswater.php

**Quest Name:** "Gathering Elements: Water"

**Quest Giver:** Everna Delestrod (Aalishai: Palace of Embers)

**Request Phrase:** "take advantage"

**Prerequisites:** Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, and Gathering Elements: Air

**Task Type:** Solo | **Time Limit:** Unlimited | **Lockout:** 30 Minutes | **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objective

- "Deliver 10 Essence of Elemental Water to Everna Delestrod 0/10"

## Instructions

Defeat water-related creatures in the Southern section of the zone to obtain Essence of Elemental Water drops. Return collected essences to Everna Delestrod at her marked location.

## Reward

- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold
- Achievement: Mercenary of Aalishai: Palace of Embers
