# Royal Visits

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/royalvisits.php

## Overview

- **Quest Name:** Royal Visits
- **Quest Giver:** Sky Orchid Understanding (Esianti: Palace of the Winds)
- **Zone:** Esianti: Palace of the Winds
- **Request Phrase:** continue
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objectives (in order)

1. "Get access to the queen of the efreeti. 0/1 (Aalishai: Palace of Embers)"
2. "Speak with Heart of Flawless Brass and convince her to stop the war. 0/1"
3. "Speak with Moon Serf of the Harmonious Heavens and try to obtain the muhbis. 0/1"
4. "Deliver the muhbis to the efreeti queen. 0/1 (Aalishai: Palace of Embers)"

## Walkthrough Steps

**Step 1:** Turn in Windseal to Dirgeful Ruby Blade in Aalishai, proceed through dialogue, say "ready" to zone into instance.

**Step 2:** Inside instance, say "in exchange" to Heart of Flawless Brass.

**Step 3:** Return to Esianti; visit Sky Orchid Understanding, obtain instance, say "ready" to enter, then say "ring with garnets and rubies muhbis" to Moon Serf of Harmonious Heavens.

**Step 4:** Return to Dirgeful Ruby Blade in Aalishai, enter instance, say "ready," turn in Ring to Heart of Flawless Brass.

## Items

- Windseal (turned in to Dirgeful Ruby Blade)
- muhbis / Ring (turned in to Heart of Flawless Brass)

## Rewards

- Experience and "212 Platinum, 5 Gold"

## Player Comments

- "in" and "exchange" are minimum keywords for Step 2
- "if in a group everyone must be in the zone for quest credit"
