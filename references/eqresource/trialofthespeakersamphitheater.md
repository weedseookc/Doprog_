# Trial of the Speaker's Amphitheater

A group trial in **The Plane of Smoke**, part of the *Trials of Smoke* instance
introduced in the EverQuest expansion **The Burning Lands (TBL)**. Completing all
five Trials of Smoke unlocks the static Plane of Smoke and Empyr.

**Objective:** "Defeat any trial that you have not already defeated and claim your
reward. 0/1"

This trial is one of five inside the parent *Trials of Smoke* solo task (quest
giver **Airbound Skystone** / portal NPCs near the Plane of Smoke portal; 30-minute
lockout on the parent). The five trials are: Trial of the Eternal Cyclone, **Trial
of the Speaker's Amphitheater**, Trial of the Wending Ways, Trial of Three, and
Trial of the Ashes of Rusted Cliff's Glory.

## Arena Layout

A circular amphitheater with a central floor where trash waves spawn and **four
elevated platforms** at the cardinal quadrants, each holding one mini-boss. A
single elevated final-boss platform is reached only after all four minis are dead.

- **NW** — Sunbird of the Dawn (red)
- **NE** — Diamond Earthshaker (orange)
- **SE** — Alabaster Moonbreeze (blue)
- **SW** — Grinning Monsoon (purple)

## The Sigil (Clicky) System

This is the core mechanic. **Trash waves drop sigil clickies that teleport players
up to the named mini-boss platforms.** You cannot reach a mini-boss platform
without clicking its matching sigil.

- Trash spawns in **timed waves**; the mobs drop the port clickies ("they drop
  clickies to port up to named").
- **"there are 3 waves between each named"** — three waves of two mobs each
  (six mobs) between named phases.
- **Wait to use the sigils until the trash waves are done.** Clicking early can
  pull a named into an unfinished wave and risk failing the event.
- Sigils come in **four colors**, each porting to a specific quadrant/boss:

| Sigil Name | Color | Mini-Boss | Quadrant |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sigil of the Soaring Heart** | Blue | Alabaster Moonbreeze | SE |
| **Sigil of the Golden Flame** | Red | Sunbird of the Dawn | NW |
| **Sigil of the Darkening Depths** | Purple | Grinning Monsoon | SW |
| **Sigil of the Hardening Heart** | Orange | Diamond Earthshaker | NE |
| **Sigil of the Overlord of Ash** | — | **Final boss platform** | Center / final |

- **Only three players can go up to each mini.** Send the **tank and melee** up
  with the sigils; "there will not be enough for a full group so decide who fights
  the bosses."
- **Make sure the three players who click use the same colored clicky** so they all
  arrive at the same platform.
- **Healers can heal from below** the platforms (line-of-sight from the floor/wall),
  so healers do not need to consume a sigil.
- Each **mini-boss drops two sigils** when killed. Do **NOT** click the mini sigils
  until the first three minis are dead — collect them first.
- The group should **collect all the clickies before any are used.**

### Failure / wipe rules (parent instance + this trial)

- Engaging **multiple minis simultaneously fails the event.** Fight one at a time.
- **Any death in the group causes distributed sigils to disappear** — typically
  forcing a restart of the mission.
- The **roaming storm cannot be targeted or killed** — it must be avoided.
- The adds **cannot be rooted, mezzed, or pacified.**

## The Four Named Mini-Bosses

Named spawn in **pairs**, and which two spawn together appears to be **random**
each run (per Escian). Each color/quadrant is fixed to its sigil:

1. **Alabaster Moonbreeze** — blue, SE, Sigil of the Soaring Heart
2. **Sunbird of the Dawn** — red, NW, Sigil of the Golden Flame
3. **Grinning Monsoon** — purple, SW, Sigil of the Darkening Depths
4. **Diamond Earthshaker** — orange, NE, Sigil of the Hardening Heart

### "Flung around the room" mechanic (verbatim)

A knockback/displacement triggers on mini-boss deaths:

- After the **first** mini dies: **"all members will be flung around the room."**
- The displacement repeats on the **second** mini death (flung again).
- After the **fourth** mini boss dies: **"Once the fourth mini boss is dead you
  will get flung all over the room."**

Position so the knockback does not throw players off platforms or into the storm.
Keeping the minis "toward the edge of the platform toward the wall" helps healer
line-of-sight (Satellite).

### Tumbling Boulder (verbatim)

> "The Tumbling Boulder spawns during the time in-between the first named death in
> the pair, and the second named death in the pair. Avoid it (even on the
> platforms)."

The boulder is the basis of the **Tumble Dry** achievement and must be dodged. It
can hit players who are up on the platforms, not just those on the floor.

## The Final Boss

After all four minis are dead (and you are flung across the room), the
**Sigil of the Overlord of Ash** becomes the port to the final encounter:

> "Sigil of the Overlord of Ash takes you to the final boss."

- The **final sigil is clickable by all group members** (unlike the mini sigils,
  which are limited to three players each).
- The boss's published proper name is **not listed on the EQ Resource page**; he is
  referred to by the sigil — the **"Overlord of Ash."** (No distinct mob name is
  published on the primary source.)

### Heal-to-full / leash mechanic (the key gotcha)

The boss **regenerates to full (100%) health** if anyone in the party is not up on
his platform. Per Battileur, the boss kept healing back to full around the 50%
mark; per Riou's diagnosis:

> "Were any of your toons or mercs not up top with him? Believe this makes him
> 'leash' and regen to 100"

**Every combatant — including mercenaries — must be on the final platform with the
boss**, or he leashes and heals to full. Bring all toons and mercs up before
engaging.

## Achievements

- **Trials of Smoke (Group)** — meta achievement (10 pts); completing all five
  Trials of Smoke grants access to The Plane of Smoke (static) and Empyr. Trial of
  the Speaker's Amphitheater is one required component.
- **A Matter of Timing** — tied to killing the named pair within the timing window
  (kill the pair "within one minute of each other").
- **Following Orders** — tied to killing the paired named in the correct (alpha)
  order. Per Escian: **"kill the pair in the alpha order and if possible, within
  one minute of each other."**
- **Tumble Dry** — avoid the Tumbling Boulder.
- **All Alone** — solo/six-box style achievement demonstrated in Tucoh's video.

All the timing/order achievements can be obtained in a single run (Escian). Because
the named pairs are random, plan kill order per pair on the fly.

## Strategy Summary (from official walkthrough + krushor + Satellite + Escian)

1. Hail **Waves of Saffron Sky** and say `prepared`.
2. Clear timed trash waves (three waves of two mobs between each named). Collect the
   colored sigil clickies but **do not click them mid-wave.**
3. When a named phase opens, send **tank + melee (three players max)** up using the
   **same-colored** sigil. Healers heal from below.
4. Fight minis **one at a time** — never engage two simultaneously (instant fail).
5. Mind the **knockback** on mini deaths and **avoid the Tumbling Boulder** between
   paired named deaths (also on platforms).
6. For achievements, kill the paired named in **alpha order within ~1 minute** of
   each other.
7. Each mini drops two sigils; collect all and don't click the mini sigils until the
   first three minis are dead.
8. After the fourth mini (and final flinging), use the **Sigil of the Overlord of
   Ash**.
9. **Get the entire party AND all mercenaries onto the final platform** before/while
   fighting the final boss, or he leashes and heals to 100%.
10. Open the chest to complete.

> Watch: any **group death wipes your collected sigils**; the **roaming storm is
> unkillable** and must be avoided; adds **cannot be rooted/mezzed/pacified.**
