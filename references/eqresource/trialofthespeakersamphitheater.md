# Trial of the Speaker's Amphitheater

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/trialofthespeakersamphitheater.php

## Quest Details

- **Quest Giver / Starting NPC:** Waves of Saffron Sky (located in The Plane of Smoke)
- **Zone:** The Plane of Smoke
- **Request Phrase:** "prepared"
- **Requirements:** Must be inside the Trials of Smoke instance
- **Time Limit:** 6 Hours
- **Task Type:** Group, 1 Minimum, 6 Maximum Players
- **Lockout:** 60 Hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objective

"Defeat any trial that you have not already defeated and claim your reward. 0/1"

## Task Steps

Speak with Waves of Saffron Sky to initiate. The trial involves defeating timed waves of mobs that drop clickies to port to named bosses. The walkthrough states: "there will not be enough for a full group so decide who fights the bosses."

Four named mini-bosses spawn across platforms:
- Alabaster Moonbreeze (Sigil of the Soaring Heart - blue, SE)
- Sunbird of the Dawn (Sigil of the Golden Flame - red, NW)
- Grinning Monsoon (Sigil of the Darkening Depths - purple, SW)
- Diamond Earthshaker (Sigil of the Hardening Heart - orange, NE)

Strategy: "Wait to use the sigils until the trash waves are done, there are 3 waves between" bosses. After defeating all four minis, use the final sigil to reach the ultimate boss.

- Experience
- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold
- Chest loot options (Blue Sky, Diminished Inhabited Muhbis, Lesser Spellbound Lamp, others)
