# Prince Ralaifin

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/princeralaifin.php

## Quest Giver
**Horizon Blighted Sage** (located in Empyr: Realms of Ash)

## Request Phrase
"gathered"

## Zone-In Phrase
"ready"

## Requirements
Must complete: Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, and Fire and Fury quests.

## Quest Properties
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 6 hours
- **Lockout:** 5 hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Task Objectives (IN ORDER)
1. "Kill 4 Ralaifin Ecclesiastic 0/4 (Empyr: Realms of Ash)"
2. "Open the chest 0/1 (Empyr: Realms of Ash)"

## Combat Mechanics (from Walkthrough)
- Four named mobs with individual abilities (mana drain, AC debuff)
- Mobs may run from group when affected
- General emote trigger: "The prayers to Prince Ralaifin are reaching rapture" - requires hiding from line of sight or taking direct damage
- Spider adds spawn during fight
- Named mobs attack from within fire auras
