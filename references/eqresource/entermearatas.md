# Enter Mearatas

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/entermearatas.php

## Overview

- **Quest Name:** Enter Mearatas
- **Quest Giver:** Blazing Sorrows Darkness or Automatically in Tyrant of Fire
- **Zone:** Aalishai: Palace of Embers
- **Request Phrase:** "require"
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Objectives (in order)

1. Find a way into Mearatas 0/1
2. Convince Glance of Sky to tell you the name of someone 0/1
3. Convince Obsidian Bitterness to tell you the name of someone 0/1
4. Convince Ivory Flume to tell you the name of someone 0/1

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

**Step 1:** Say "enter mearatas" to earth mobs on Eastern side of zone. *Note: Steps 2-4 execute in random order.*

**Step 2 - Glance of Sky Task:**
- Defeat and loot head from Dread Last Guardian
- Turn head to Glance of Sky
- *May require faction or sneak ability*

**Step 3 - Obsidian Bitterness Task:**
- Hail Obsidian Bitterness
- Hail an Udex; correct one responds: "I will not speak about anyone appearing before me in my capacity as Udex."
- Turn note to that Udex (receives "Opened Copper Note")
- Return "Opened Copper Note" to Obsidian Bitterness

**Step 4 - Ivory Flume Task:**
- Defeat and loot head from Hidden Dust
- Turn head to Eyes Unconquered
- Turn stone to Ivory Flume

## NPCs / Mobs

- Glance of Sky
- Obsidian Bitterness
- Ivory Flume
- Dread Last Guardian (loot head)
- Udex (multiple spawn; only correct one gives proper response)
- Eyes Unconquered (turn-in for head)
- Hidden Dust (loot head)

## Items

- head (from Dread Last Guardian) -> Glance of Sky
- note -> Udex
- "Opened Copper Note" (received from Udex) -> Obsidian Bitterness
- head (from Hidden Dust) -> Eyes Unconquered
- stone -> Ivory Flume

## Exact Emote / Dialogue Text

- Correct Udex responds: "I will not speak about anyone appearing before me in my capacity as Udex."

## Rewards

- Experience
- 425 Platinum

## Player Comments - Key Issues

- **Step 1 Bug:** Players reported it becomes unresponsive; solution was dropping/restarting quest or trying pick zones
- **Grouping Issue:** "Once a single person gets update then no one else can"
- **Pre-looting:** Items can be collected before steps unlock
- **Faction:** Indifferent or better needed for Glance of Sky unless sneaking
- **Udex Mechanics:** Multiple Udex spawn; only correct one gives proper response
