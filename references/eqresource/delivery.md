# Delivery

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/delivery.php

## Overview
- **Quest Name:** Delivery
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Unrepentant Sunrise
- **Zone:** Doomfire, the Burning Lands
- **Request Phrase:** "delivery"
- **Zone-In Phrase:** "ready"
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Time Limit:** 6 Hours
- **Lockout:** 5 Hours
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Requirements (prerequisite tasks)
Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, Contract of War, All Hail the King, Royal Visits, Tyrant of Fire, Enter Mearatas, Serving Another Master, Earthen Dirge, Mold Seeker, and Relic Raider.

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "You will have to fight your way to the throne room to speak with Fennin Ro. 0/3"
2. "Deliver the duende mold to Fennin Ro. 0/1"
3. "Open the chest. 0/1"

## Walkthrough / Mechanics
- Defeat jopal see-invis mobs at zone-in.
- Can invis and run to Tables in southeast.
- Defeat Guardian of Doomfire (applies "Doom" debuff to a random player).
- Continue east to defeat 2 Snails.
- Turn in mold to Fennin Ro at the throne room.
- Open the resulting chest.

## Items
- duende mold (delivered to Fennin Ro)

## Turn-In
Fennin Ro (throne room) - deliver the duende mold.

## Rewards
- Experience
- 318 Platinum, 7 Gold, 5 Silver
- 143x Fettered Ifrit Coins
- Scintillating Ribbon of Power (chest loot)

## Player Comments
- One player reported the mold was "removed from your inventory upon zoning".
- Multiple reports of Fennin Ro not spawning.
- Solution noted: "destroy it and let Unrepentant Sunrise give you one" if already possessed.
