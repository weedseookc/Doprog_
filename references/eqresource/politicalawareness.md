# Political Awareness

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/politicalawareness.php

## Quest Info
- **Quest Name:** Political Awareness
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Rianar Gadliun (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)
- **Request Phrase:** "guess"
- **Time Limit:** 24 Hours
- **Task Type:** Group (1-6 players)
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Task Objectives (In Order)
1. "Discover some of the history of the leadership of the djinn. 0/1"
2. "Discover how rulership change happens in Esianti. 0/1"
3. "Find out what the citizens think about current rulership. 0/1"
4. "Obviously some of the citizens do not care who their ruler is. 0/1"
5. "Defeat the angry elementals. 0/3"
6. "Report what you have learned to Rianar 0/1"

## Walkthrough Steps
**Step 1:** Hail NPCs (dervishes, djinn, mephits) in the Plane of Tranquility half of the zone until one provides text.

**Step 2:** Near Great Sky Ocean and northern islands, say "what about wind of the shining void" for blue clickable text.

**Step 3:** In same areas, say "what about the current rulership" to spawn 3 angry elementals that aggro.

**Step 4:** Say "what about moon serf of the harmonious heavens" until updating.

**Step 5:** Defeat the three spawned elementals.

**Step 6:** Return and hail Rianar Gadliun.
