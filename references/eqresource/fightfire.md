# Fight Fire
Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/fightfire.php

## Start NPC
- **Grieving Soul Scent** (location coordinates not provided in source)
- Zone: Stratos: Zephyr's Flight
- Request Phrase: "defeat"
- Zone-In Phrase: "ready"

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "Defeat the invaders. 0/12 (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)"
2. "Defeat the Efreeti lieutenants. 0/4 (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)"
3. "Receive judgment. 0/1 (Stratos: Zephyr's Flight)"

## Mechanics
- First phase: Talk to NPCs; they aggro and spawn a second wave.
- Second phase: Four linked efreeti lieutenants spawn; "If they go inactive Balance the active ones to make them re-activate".
- Final phase: Say 'grieving soul scent' to Evasion Flame Desires to complete.

## Turn-In
- Say 'grieving soul scent' to Evasion Flame Desires

## Rewards
- 212 Platinum, 5 Gold
- Airbound Skystone
- 96x Fettered Ifrit Coins

## Player Comments
- Mobs are snare-capable and rootable.
- Four lieutenants cannot be separated and are aggro-linked.
- Mobs periodically go inactive unless another is being actively damaged.
- Melee hits recorded at "up to a little over 30k" with occasional 25k quads.
- Mobs resist root-based crowd control.
- Mezzable but problematic for damage progression.
