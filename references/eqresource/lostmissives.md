# Lost Missives

Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/lostmissives.php

## Overview
- **Quest Name:** Lost Missives
- **Quest Giver / Start NPC:** Emli Widgetton (in Mearatas: The Stone Demesne)
- **Zone:** Mearatas: The Stone Demesne
- **Request Phrase:** "messengers"
- **Task Type:** Solo
- **Time Limit:** Unlimited
- **Lockout:** 30 Minutes
- **Repeatable:** Yes

## Requirements (prerequisite tasks)
Soldier of Air, Fight Fire, Any Trials of Smoke, Prisoner's Dilemma, Palace of Embers, Brass Palace, Key to the Kingdom, Contract of War, All Hail the King, Royal Visits, Tyrant of Fire, Enter Mearatas, and Serving Another Master.

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "Kill Envoys 0/6 (Mearatas: The Stone Demesne)" - Defeat envoys found throughout the zone
2. "Return to Emli 0/1 (Mearatas: The Stone Demesne)" - Return to and Hail Emli

## Turn-In
Return to and Hail Emli (Emli Widgetton).

## Rewards / Achievement
- Achievement: Mercenary of Mearatas: The Stone Demesne
