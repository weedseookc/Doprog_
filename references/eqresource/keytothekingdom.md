# Key to the Kingdom
Source: https://tbl.eqresource.com/keytothekingdom.php

## Quest Giver / Start NPC
- **Dusky Iron Meditation**
- Zone: Stratos: Zephyr's Flight
- Request Phrase: "accept"

## Objectives (In Order)
1. "Find Dusky Iron Meditation's servant, Unfettered Stone. 0/1"
2. "Return the remains of Unfettered Stone to Dusky Iron Meditation. 0/1"
3. "Find out how Unfettered Stone died. 0/1"
4. "Return the gemstone heart of Unfettered Stone to Dusky Iron Meditation. 0/1"
5. "Bring Dusky Iron Meditation's evidence to an Udex and convince them to hear his case. 0/1"
6. "Give the warrant stone to Dusky Iron Meditation so that the contract can be completed. 0/1"
7. "Speak with Dusky Iron Meditation. 0/1"
8. "Tell Great Sky Ocean that you have the key to enter Esianti. 0/1"

## Key Items & Locations
- **Ground Spawn:** Pile of Rubble (Unfettered Stone's remains)
- **Encounter Mobs:** upset dervish, hidden mephit, languishing courier (spawn one per person; defeat to loot Gemstone Heart)
- **Turn-ins:** Clear Gemstone (to Udex), Warrant Stone (to Dusky Iron Meditation)

## Turn-In NPC
- Dusky Iron Meditation (warrant stone), then tell Great Sky Ocean

## Reward
- 283 Platinum, 3 Gold, 3 Silver, 3 Copper + Experience
